Magic Mail is a productivity-focused Thunderbird add-on that automatically re-attaches files from the original email when you reply or reply-all, provides mail scheduling, and offers quick response templates.

### Key Features

1. **Auto-Attach Original Files on Reply**
   - Automatically copies non-signature attachments from the original email when you click Reply or Reply All.
   - Can be toggled on/off anytime from the main toolbar popup or Settings.
   - Manual one-click **Reply with Attachments** and **Reply All with Attachments** actions are always available in the message header toolbar, even when Auto-Attach is turned off.

2. **Mail Scheduling, Recurring Emails & Delivery History (with Attachments)**
   - Schedule any composed email (including its attachments) for one-time or recurring (**Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**) delivery.
   - View, reschedule (edit delivery time or repeat interval), **Pause/Resume**, or cancel/remove upcoming scheduled emails directly from the main **Magic Mail** toolbar popup.
   - Review past deliveries (`Sent` / `Failed`) in the **History** tab and **Open Draft** or **Reschedule** any previous email in one click.

3. **Quick Response Templates**
   - Create, edit, delete, import (JSON), and export (JSON) reusable email templates in Settings.
   - Insert templates into any compose window via the **Advance Options** compose toolbar popup (with instant search) or by right-clicking in the compose body (**Quick Templates** context menu).

4. **Light & Dark Theme Support**
   - Includes a built-in Light and Dark mode toggle synchronized across popups and Settings.

---

### Where to Find Magic Mail (Entry Points)

1. **Main Toolbar Button (`Magic Mail`)**
   - Located on Thunderbird's main toolbar.
   - Opens the **Magic Mail** popup where you can:
     - Toggle **Auto-Attach Files** on or off.
     - Check whether **Mail Scheduling** and **Quick Templates** are active.
     - View, **Edit** (reschedule), or **Remove** your **Scheduled Messages**.
     - Open **Settings** or switch between Light and Dark mode.

2. **Message Header Toolbar Button (`Reply With`)**
   - Located in the message display header toolbar when reading an email.
   - Provides **Reply with Attachments** and **Reply All with Attachments** buttons to reply with the original email's attachments on demand.

3. **Compose Window Toolbar Button (`Advance Options`)**
   - Located in the compose window toolbar when writing an email.
   - Opens a two-tab popup (enabled in Settings):
     - **Schedule**: Pick a future date and time and click **Schedule Delivery** (preserves all compose details and attachments).
     - **Templates**: Search and click any saved template to insert it at the cursor.

4. **Compose Body Context Menu (`Quick Templates`)**
   - Right-click inside the email compose body when **Quick Templates** is enabled and you have at least one saved template to insert a template directly.

5. **Add-on Settings (`Tools -> Add-ons and Themes -> Magic Mail -> Preferences`)**
   - Configure **Auto-Attach Original Files**, **Mail Scheduling**, **Notify on Success**, **Quick Template**, and **Console Debug Logs**, and manage your saved templates.
